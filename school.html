Why Whiskered Woods

Sourdough quality, without the sourdough commitment.

🌾
Long-fermented sourdough

Every sourdough product starts with Bob, our sourdough starter born in the Bahamas. The long fermentation develops real flavor — the kind that takes 12+ hours you don't have to spend in the kitchen.

📋
You know exactly what's in it

Full ingredient lists and allergen information on every label. Bread flour, water, starter, salt, olive oil, honey. Real ingredients, no shortcuts.

🏠
Made by your neighbors

We're a family in Zeeland — a block from ZCS. When you order from us, you're supporting a real family and a real homeschool business project, not a commercial bakery.

👩‍🍳
Made to order, never sitting on a shelf

We bake after orders close. Nothing is pre-made and waiting — your focaccia comes out of the oven Friday morning, and you pick it up Friday afternoon.

✅
Fully compliant cottage food operation

Licensed under Michigan Cottage Food Law, registered with the state, and fully insured. Our kids completed the MSU Extension cottage food safety course before we sold our first loaf.

How it works

1
Browse and order Saturday morning

Orders open every Saturday at 9am. Shop the menu at hotplate.com/whiskeredwoods and place your order in a few minutes.

2
We bake after orders close

Orders close Tuesday at 11:45pm. We build our sourdough batches and bake Wednesday through Friday so everything is fresh. Nothing goes in the oven until we know what you ordered.

3
Pick up Friday 3–6pm in Zeeland

Right near Zeeland Christian School. Exact address comes with your order confirmation. In and out in two minutes — we have everything ready and labeled.

4
Spin the prize wheel

Every order in September gets a spin at pickup. Win extra baked goods, loyalty points, or surprise goodies. Loyalty points add up to discounts on future orders.

A family kitchen.
A real homeschool project.

The Whiskered Woods is run by a mom and two kids in Zeeland. Our 13-year-old makes every cookie. Our 11-year-old is developing tea blends and scones and helps with photography. Both completed the MSU Extension cottage food safety course before we opened.

Our sourdough starter Bob was born in the Bahamas in January 2025 and has traveled more miles than most starters ever will. He lives in our kitchen now and makes bread every week. Every loaf starts with him.

This is our homeschool this summer. These are our kids' real skills. This is our actual life. 🦊
