This isn't catering

We're not a food service and don't serve food at your event. This is a baked goods market table — customers order ahead from an online menu and pick up at your shop, or walk up and buy directly at the table. Either way, it's product they take home, not something served on-site.

We sell directly to each customer ourselves, as required under Michigan cottage food law.

How it works

1

Reach out with your event

Tell us the event, the date, and roughly how many people you expect. We'll confirm we're free that day.

2

We design a themed menu

Tell us the vibe — your event, your shop's own name and personality, whatever fits — and we'll suggest flavor pairings. You don't have to have it all figured out; we'll keep names original and clear of anything copyrighted.

3

Early bird pre-orders open, and we bake extra

We share a public pre-order link ahead of the event so early birds are guaranteed their order — often at a small discount for ordering ahead. We bring extra for walk-up sales at the table too.

4

We set up, sell, and pack out

We bring our own table and display, sell directly to your customers, and take anything unsold with us. Standard events include up to 2 hours of table time — longer than that, just ask and we'll work it out.

What we'd love from you

—

A spot for our table

We bring a compact, self-contained setup — roughly an 8-foot table's worth of space — not a full market stall. Just need a corner or spot near the action for the length of the event.

—

Advance notice of your event theme

So we have time to design and test a menu that actually fits.

—

Real promotion, not just a mention

A few posts across your channels in the lead-up, your event page, and in-store signage — the more people know ahead of time, the more they pre-order, and the better this works for both of us. On our end, the drop will also be listed publicly on our own ordering page and shared with our regular customers — genuine cross-promotion both ways.

Your first event with us is free to try — no minimum to worry about. We just ask that you genuinely promote it alongside us, so it's worth planning around your date. If it goes well, we're glad to schedule another — and can talk about a permanent spot down the road if that's a fit.
