How it works

1

One person reaches out

A group organizer shares the theme (a book, a campaign, an occasion), the preferred date, and a pickup or drop-off location — a quick message, call, or email works.

2

We confirm the date

We check our baking calendar and confirm we can take it, or suggest a nearby date that works better on our end.

3

The organizer confirms the minimum

We'll share example pricing for your theme. Since you know your group best, the organizer confirms ahead of time that people are on board with the $100 minimum before we design or build anything.

4

We design your menu, and the link opens

We build your 3–4 item menu — a mix of bread or focaccia, scones, cookies, tea blends, and dry mixes (herb blends, pancake mix, beer or soda bread mix) — and send your private order link.

5

Pickup at your agreed spot

Everyone orders and pays individually, or one person orders for the group. We drop off, or the organizer picks up everyone's order, at the location you've agreed on together.

Timeline & minimums

Everything below is counted backward from your pickup date — so "7 days before pickup" means 7 days before your group picks up, not 7 days from today.

—

2–3 weeks before pickup: request your drop

Reach out with your theme and preferred date, and confirm with your group ahead of time that they're on board with the $100 minimum. We'll confirm we're free that day, or offer an alternate.

—

Before anything is designed: organizer confirms

We share example pricing and the organizer gives us the go-ahead. We only build the menu and open ordering once that's confirmed.

—

7 days before pickup: order window opens

Your private link goes live and your group starts ordering.

—

3 days before pickup: orders close

The menu locks so we can start long-fermentation sourdough and get everything baked fresh for pickup.
